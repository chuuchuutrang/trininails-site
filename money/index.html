The main rule

Give every dollar a job: spend, protect, or grow. If it has no job, it tends to disappear.

A sensible order

Build the floor before the ceiling.

This is a starter sequence, not a law. Your debts, benefits, dependents, health, and income stability can change the order.

  1. 01
    Cover essentialsHousing, food, health, transport, minimum payments.
  2. 02
    Build a starter bufferEven a small cash reserve can stop a surprise from becoming debt.
  3. 03
    Capture the matchIf your employer matches a 401(k), learn the rules and collect the full benefit.
  4. 04
    Attack costly debtHigh interest can compound against you faster than investments grow for you.
  5. 05
    Strengthen the emergency fundKeep it safe, separate, and easy to reach.
  6. 06
    Invest for the long termUse tax-advantaged accounts where they fit, automate, and start early.

Two savings targets

Save what remains—before it finds a reason to leave.

These percentages apply after essential expenses, not to total income. Pick a target that is demanding but sustainable.

Strong target50%

Save half of what remains.

An ambitious benchmark that still leaves room for life today.

Maximum savings target90%

Save or invest almost all of what remains.

01Prices moveInflation—and inflationary spending

ELI5: inflation means the same money buys fewer things. If a snack rises from $1 to $1.10, your dollar did not shrink—the menu did.

What

A broad rise in prices over time.

Why it matters

Cash that earns less than inflation loses buying power.

Inflationary spending

If people expect higher prices and rush to buy now, today’s demand can push prices higher still. Wages and prices do not always rise together.

02Safe cashHYSA

What

A high-yield savings account: ordinary savings with a more competitive variable rate.

Why

Your short-term cash earns interest while remaining available.

When

Emergency savings and money needed in roughly the next few years.

Check the APY, fees, withdrawal access, and that the bank is FDIC-insured. Deposit insurance is generally automatic up to at least $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, per ownership category.

03Protect the planEmergency savings

A dedicated cash reserve keeps a car repair, medical bill, or income gap from automatically becoming expensive debt. Start with a reachable buffer; then build toward the amount your own risks require. Keep it safe, accessible, separate, and boring.

There is no universal perfect number. Irregular income, dependents, health needs, and job risk usually call for a larger cushion.

04Get helpAutomated and managed planning

Fidelity Go is a robo-advisory account: answer questions, fund it, and Fidelity selects and rebalances a portfolio. As of August 2026, there is no minimum to open, investing starts at $10, the advisory fee is $0 below $25,000 and 0.35% a year at $25,000 or more.

Fidelity Wealth Management is a broader human-advice relationship for more complex needs and generally larger balances. Compare services, fees, minimums, conflicts, and whether you really need ongoing management before choosing any provider.

A managed account can simplify decisions; it cannot remove market risk or guarantee returns.

05Retirement choiceRoth vs. traditional IRA

Roth IRA

Contribute after-tax money. Qualified withdrawals can be tax-free. Often attractive when your tax rate is lower now, but income limits apply.

Traditional IRA

Contributions may be deductible now; withdrawals are generally taxable later. Deductibility can depend on income and workplace-plan coverage.

The combined 2026 IRA contribution limit is $7,500, plus a $1,100 catch-up at age 50+. “Roth or traditional?” is often answered with tax rates, eligibility, flexibility, and a mix—not ideology.

06Workplace investing401(k)

A retirement account offered through work. Contributions can be traditional (tax break now) or Roth (qualified withdrawals tax-free later), depending on the plan.

Company matchingYour employer may add money when you contribute, according to the plan’s formula and vesting rules.

Get the full matchIf applicable, contribute enough each pay period to receive the full company match.

The 2026 employee contribution limit is $24,500. Age 50+ catch-up is generally $8,000; ages 60–63 may have an $11,250 catch-up. Limits and eligibility can change.

07Use timeFront-load investing

If money is already available for a long-term goal, investing earlier gives it more time in the market. Vanguard research found lump-sum investing historically beat temporarily holding cash and averaging in roughly two-thirds of the time.

But sequence matters: first protect essentials, avoid high-interest debt traps, and keep near-term money safe. Investing can fall. If a 401(k) match is paid per paycheck, maxing out too early may also miss later matching dollars unless the plan has a true-up.

Front-loading is not predicting the market. It is giving long-term money more time—while accepting that earlier exposure also means earlier risk.

The quiet advantage

Earn. Keep. Protect. Grow. Repeat.

You do not need to master finance in one day. Make one good money decision easier to repeat, then let consistency and time help.
